# Research Scope

- Mode: literature-first
- Acquisition: behavior
- Variant: baseline identity negative priming
- Participant language: Chinese instructions; nonverbal abstract-shape stimuli
- Primary protocol: Friedman and Miyake (2004), shape-matching task
- Primary contrast: correct probe RT in negative-priming pairs minus correct probe RT in control-distractor pairs

The implementation preserves the cited button-press shape-matching procedure rather
than translating the cited English word-naming variant. This avoids voice-key and
language-frequency confounds while retaining the ignored-repetition manipulation.
